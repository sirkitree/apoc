# NOTICE

This file records, for every translation file in this repository, the specific
basis on which it is believed to be freely redistributable. It exists because a
blanket claim in a README is not a per-file basis, and because at least one file
in this collection turned out not to be public domain at all (see
[Removed files](#removed-files) below).

For the licensing of the material written *for* this repository -- the per-book
`README.md` essays, the root `README.md`, the retranslation samples, and the
creative work in `books/Tobit/` -- see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). That license covers
the original material only; it makes no claim over the translations.

This is a good-faith record compiled by reading the files and their sources. It
is not legal advice. Where a basis could not be verified it is marked
**UNVERIFIED**, and those files are listed together in
[Files needing rights review](#files-needing-rights-review).

## How to read the "Basis" column

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Dedication** | The translator or publisher has explicitly placed the work in the public domain. The dedication is quoted or cited. |
| **Age (US)** | Published in the United States before 1930, or first published abroad before 1930 and now out of US copyright by date of publication. |
| **Age (US + UK)** | Out of copyright by publication date in the US *and* by the author's date of death plus 70 years in the UK/EU. |
| **Licensed** | Still under copyright, but distributed under a license that permits redistribution. The license is named. |
| **UNVERIFIED** | No dedication found, no translator or date established. Treated as unresolved. |

A note on the **King James Version**: the KJV is in the public domain in the
United States and in most of the world. In the United Kingdom it is subject to a
perpetual Crown patent administered by Cambridge University Press, Oxford
University Press and the Queen's/King's Printer. Reproduction outside the UK is
unaffected. Every KJV file below is drawn from eBible.org's *King James Version +
Apocrypha* edition, which eBible.org distributes as public domain.

A note on the **Project Gutenberg** files: the Douay-Rheims text in these files
is public domain by age. The files retain Project Gutenberg's own header and
footer, which carry PG's separate trademark terms; those terms apply to the
Project Gutenberg boilerplate, not to the scripture.
