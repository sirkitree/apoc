# Wisdom of Solomon - Chapter 1
## Modern Plain English Translation

*Style: Contemporary, accessible prose in the spirit of the NLT. Translated from the KJV text into everyday modern English for clarity and readability.*

---

1. You leaders of the world -- love what is right. Think about the Lord with sincere hearts, and look for him with an uncomplicated trust.

2. He lets himself be found by those who don't put him to the test, and he reveals himself to those who trust him.

3. Twisted thinking pushes you away from God. And when people test his power, it only exposes how foolish they are.

4. Wisdom won't enter a heart that plots evil, and it won't live in a body given over to sin.

5. God's holy Spirit of guidance will run from dishonesty. It pulls away from confused and senseless thinking, and it leaves whenever wrongdoing shows up.

6. Wisdom is a spirit that loves people -- but it won't let a person who insults God off the hook. God sees into the deepest parts of who you are. He truly observes your heart and hears every word you say.

7. The Spirit of the Lord fills the entire world. He holds all things together and is aware of every word that is spoken.

8. So no one who says wrong things can hide from him. Justice will find them -- it won't just pass them by.

9. The plans of wicked people will be investigated. The things they've said will reach the Lord and expose what they've really done.

10. God's attentive ear hears everything. Even quiet grumbling doesn't escape his notice.

11. So watch out for complaining -- it does you no good. And stop talking behind people's backs. No word is so secret that it just disappears. A lying mouth destroys the soul.

12. Don't chase after death by living the wrong way. Don't bring destruction on yourselves by what you do.

13. God didn't create death. He takes no pleasure in destroying the living.

14. He created everything so that it would thrive. The things he made for the world were meant to be life-giving. There was no deadly poison built into them, and death was never meant to rule on earth.

15. (Because righteousness lasts forever.)

16. But ungodly people invited death in through their own actions and words. They thought death was their friend, so they wasted away to nothing. They made a deal with death -- and they got exactly what they deserved.
