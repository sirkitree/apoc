# Wisdom of Solomon, Chapter 1
## Literary Modern English Translation

*Style: Elevated literary prose in the tradition of the NRSV and Robert Alter --
dignified, precise, and attentive to the parallelism and rhetorical weight of the
original. Modern vocabulary is used throughout, but the register remains formal
and the cadences deliberate, preserving the gravity of the source text.*

---

1. Love righteousness, you who judge the earth;
set your mind upon the Lord with integrity,
and seek him in sincerity of heart.

2. For he is found by those who do not put him to the test,
and he reveals himself to those who do not distrust him.

3. For perverse thoughts separate one from God,
and his power, when tested, exposes the foolish.

4. For wisdom will not enter a soul bent on evil,
nor dwell in a body enslaved to sin.

5. For the holy spirit of discipline flees from deceit
and withdraws from thoughts devoid of understanding,
and will not remain where unrighteousness intrudes.

6. For wisdom is a spirit that loves humanity,
yet she will not hold a blasphemer guiltless for his words --
for God is witness to the inmost self,
a truthful observer of the heart,
and one who hears every word the tongue utters.

7. For the Spirit of the Lord fills the world,
and that which holds all things together
has knowledge of every voice.

8. Therefore no one who speaks unrighteously shall escape notice,
nor shall justice, in its reckoning, pass that one by.

9. For inquiry will be made into the counsels of the ungodly,
and a report of their words will reach the Lord
for the exposure of their lawless deeds.

10. For the ear of divine jealousy hears all things,
and the sound of murmuring does not go unheard.

11. Therefore guard yourselves against futile grumbling,
and keep your tongue from slander;
for no word spoken in secret is without consequence,
and a lying mouth destroys the soul.

12. Do not court death through the error of your ways,
nor draw destruction upon yourselves by the work of your own hands.

13. For God did not make death,
nor does he take pleasure in the ruin of the living.

14. For he created all things so that they might exist;
the generative powers of the world are wholesome,
and there is in them no poison of destruction,
nor does death hold dominion upon the earth --

15. for righteousness is immortal.

16. But the ungodly, by their deeds and words, summoned death to themselves;
counting it a friend, they wasted away,
and they made a covenant with it,
for they are fit companions for its company.
